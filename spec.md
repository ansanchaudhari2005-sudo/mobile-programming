Problem:
Students in Nepal struggle to find trustworthy part-time jobs that match their skills, location, and college schedule.

Objectives

The main objectives of PartTime Nepal are:

To connect students with part-time job opportunities in Nepal.
To help students find jobs that fit their class schedules and available time.
To provide verified employers and genuine job listings to reduce fake job opportunities.
To allow employers to find suitable student workers based on their skills and availability.
To provide information about salary, working hours, location, and job requirements clearly.
To support different types of part-time work, such as tutoring, event work, data entry, delivery, and social media management.
To help students gain work experience and earn income while continuing their education.
To create a simple and user-friendly platform for both students and employers.
